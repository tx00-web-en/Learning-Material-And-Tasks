## Agenda for Team Meeting

### 1. Introductions and Skill Assessment

Get to know your team members.

Discuss:

* What skills and experience does each member already have?
* What would each member like to learn?
* What technologies or areas would the team like to explore?
* Are there any areas where the team currently has a skills gap?

The goal is not to divide the project permanently between team members. Instead, identify areas where members can **take the lead in learning and supporting the team** during Sprint 1.

---

### 2. Brainstorm Project Ideas

Discuss and choose the type of project your team would like to build.

Consider:

* Who are the target users?
* What problem are you trying to solve?
* Why would users need this application?
* What would be the core functionality?
* What would be the minimum useful version of the product?

You may develop your own idea or use/adapt one of the [project ideas](#project-ideas) below.

---

### 3. Establish Scrum Roles

Decide who will initially serve as:

* **Product Owner**
* **Scrum Master**

The **Scrum Master role may rotate between Sprints** if the team finds this useful.

The **Product Owner should ideally remain consistent throughout the project**, because this helps maintain continuity in product vision and backlog prioritization.

The remaining team members will work as **Developers**.

Remember that Scrum roles do not prevent team members from contributing in other areas.

---

### 4. Identify Learning and Technical Responsibilities

Discuss which skills the team needs to develop during Sprint 1 in preparation for the following Sprints.

For example:

* **Figma / Prototyping:** One or two members can take the lead in learning Figma and creating the prototype.

  * A useful resource is available through [Metropolia's Library](https://metropolia.finna.fi/Record/nelli15.5850000000052946?sid=4846325074).

* **CSS / Tailwind:** One member can take the lead in reviewing CSS skills, particularly media queries, Flexbox, and responsive design. This will help the team when the prototype is converted into a React UI in Sprint 2.

  * A useful resource is available through [Metropolia's Library](https://metropolia.finna.fi/Record/nelli15.5680000000060713?sid=4846325380).

* **Trello:** Set up the team's Trello board and agree on how the team will use it to manage the Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, and tasks.

* **Other technologies:** Identify other areas the team may need to investigate.

These are **learning responsibilities, not permanent job titles**. The goal is to help the team build the knowledge needed for the next Sprints.

---

### 5. Start Your Product Backlog

Begin identifying the main functionality your application will need.

Discuss:

* Who are our users?
* What do they want to accomplish?
* What are the most important features?
* What user stories can we identify?

Create some initial user stories using:

> **As a [user], I need [function], so that [benefit].**

Add the initial stories to your Trello Product Backlog.

You do **not** need to complete the entire Product Backlog this afternoon. The backlog will evolve throughout the course.

---

### 6. Agree on How You Will Work

Before ending the session, agree on some practical ways of working.

For example:

* When will the team hold Daily Scrums?
* Which communication channel will you use?
* Where will project files be stored?
* How will you coordinate work?
* How will you make decisions?
* How will you make sure everyone contributes?
* How will you keep the Trello board updated?

Remember that the goal is to establish a way of working that you can inspect and improve during the Sprint.
