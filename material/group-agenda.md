# Autonomous Group Session — Sprint 1 Refinement & Presentation Prep

This afternoon's session is dedicated to reviewing your team dynamics, refining your backlog using **DEEP & INVEST**, finalizing your Sprint 1 deliverables, and preparing your **Sprint 1 presentation** for next week.

**Sprint Reminder:**
* **Sprint 1 Goal:** Validate the product idea, define core user stories, and create the low-fidelity prototype (e.g., in Figma).
* **Next Week:** Teams will present and demo their Sprint 1 outcomes.

---

## Agenda for Team Meeting

### 1. Team Check-in & Team Development (Tuckman's Model)

Now that your team has worked together for a week, take 10–15 minutes to reflect on your collaboration and working habits.

Discuss:
* **Where is our team right now?**
  * *Forming:* Still getting to know each other and clarifying expectations.
  * *Storming:* Experiencing friction, differing opinions, or confusion over tasks and technical choices.
  * *Norming:* Developing shared agreements, clear communication habits, and smooth collaboration.
  * *Performing:* Working autonomously, resolving issues quickly, and delivering predictably.
* **Ways of Working Check:**
  * Are our communication channels and Daily Scrums working well?
  * Is everyone contributing equally and getting support when stuck?
  * What is one agreement we can establish today to make our team stronger (*Norming*)?

---

### 2. Backlog Health Check (Applying DEEP & INVEST)

Open your team's **Trello board** and conduct a Backlog Refinement session.

#### Zoom Out: Is your Product Backlog DEEP?
* **Detailed appropriately:** Are near-term stories well-defined with clear Acceptance Criteria, while future ideas remain high-level?
* **Estimated:** Have you assigned Story Points to your top-priority items using relative sizing?
* **Emergent:** Has the backlog changed based on what you learned this week? (Add, remove, or modify items as needed).
* **Prioritized:** Is the backlog ordered in a single sequential list (1, 2, 3...) rather than having everything marked as "High Priority"?

#### Zoom In: Are your individual stories INVEST?
Pick 2–3 key user stories from your board and check:
* Are they **Independent** and **Negotiable**?
* Do they focus on **Valuable** user outcomes (not just technical setup tasks)?
* Are they **Small** enough to be delivered within a single Sprint?
* Are they **Testable** with clear Acceptance Criteria (e.g., *"Given..., When..., Then..."*)?

---

### 3. Finalize the Sprint 1 Prototype

Review the current state of your prototype (Figma, Draw.io, or chosen tool).

Ensure that:
* The core user flow is complete and testable from start to finish.
* The prototype accurately reflects the top user stories in your Sprint 1 Backlog.
* Any missing screens or edge cases (e.g., error messages, empty states) are addressed.
* The prototype is ready to be demonstrated interactively to stakeholders/classmates next week.

---

### 4. Prepare for Next Week's Sprint 1 Presentation

Next week, your team will present your Sprint 1 results. Decide how you will organize your presentation.

#### Recommended Presentation Structure (approx. 5–7 minutes):
1. **Product Vision & Target Users:** What problem does your app solve, and for whom?
2. **Scrum Roles & Team Setup:** Who served as Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers?
3. **Product Backlog & DEEP/INVEST:** Show your Trello board. Highlight how you prioritized and structured your user stories.
4. **Live Prototype Demo:** Walk through the main user journey in your prototype.
5. **Team Reflection & Next Steps:** What did the team learn during Sprint 1, and what are your main priorities for Sprint 2 (React UI)?

#### Action Items:
* Assign speaking roles so **every team member participates** in the presentation.
* Prepare a brief slide deck or structured demo script.
* Test your prototype share link to make sure permissions allow external viewing.

* Please consider using one of the following tools for your slides:
  - [Figma Slides](https://www.figma.com/slides/), 
  - [Google Slides](https://workspace.google.com/products/slides/), or
  - [Reveal.js](https://revealjs.com/)  

---

### 5. Preview & Technical Prep for Sprint 2

In Sprint 2, your team will begin converting the Figma prototype into a **React UI** (without backend logic).

Discuss briefly:
* How will your prototype be broken down into modular React components?
* Which styling approach will the team use (e.g., Tailwind CSS, plain CSS)?
* Does anyone on the team need to review React or Tailwind resources before next week?
